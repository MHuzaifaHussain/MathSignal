## span-shape

Press Check before picking a guess to check that it is refused, then try each guess. After Check the span shows as an ochre line edge to edge, or an ochre ring at the origin; the whole plane is said in words only. Then try the four presets: One zero vector draws the line from a alone, and Both zero leaves only the origin ring.
