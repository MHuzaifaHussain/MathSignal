# 0020: FunctionCurve, a Mafs board for slopes, rulers and corners

Status: accepted, 2026-11-25. The heights after Check are estimates until they are measured on a hydrated board (see Consequences).

## Context
The derivatives page teaches the slope at a point by shortening a ruler (a secant) on both sides of x = 1 on y = x² until its slope settles, then shows the tangent and the slope curve, and uses ReLU's corner at 0 to show where no single slope exists. Later calculus and activation pages reuse the same picture. The page sets the reader's flow; the board-wide rules (one ochre element, controls that are the question not rendered before Check, tray sliders as the keyboard path, shared modules) win over the page where they disagree. The ladder of rulers (2.1, 2.01, 2.001) is the lesson, so floats, which print 0.1 + 0.2 artifacts, can't carry it.

## Decision
1. **One Mafs board picked by `setup`**: `ruler-to-tangent` (y = x² at x = 1) and `corner` (ReLU at x = 0), on the shared guided modules ([0013](0013-shared-board-modules.md)). The split follows [0014](0014-vector-board.md): pure `functions.ts` (the catalog), `model.ts` (rulers, misses, corner lines, the edge test), `setups.ts`, `state.ts` (the reducer), `describe.ts` (every string) and `marks.ts` (what the plane draws), all checked by `npm run check:boards`; Mafs appears only in `CurvePlane.tsx` and `SlopeStrip.tsx`, the table is `CurveTables.tsx`.
2. **The page sets the flow; board rules win.**
   - `ruler-to-tangent`: before Check the reader picks a ruler on the six-value ladder ±1, ±0.5, ±0.25 and sets "Your slope at x = 1" (0 to 4 in quarter steps, opening at 0), Check in `retry` mode, "Show the answer" after two misses (it sets the guess to 2). After Check: the full ladder down to ±0.001, the "Point x" slider (the point's handle becomes a pointer shortcut), the stages Tangent and Slope curve, and the presets Bowl (x² at 1), S-curve (x³ at 1) and Loss ((w − 3)² at 0). Stages: Slide (the squared-paper scene, Bowl with x from 1 to 2 only, so every pencil slopes up), Ruler, Tangent, Slope curve. When a change takes Slide away, Ruler is selected and the reason is said once.
   - `corner`: the same ladder and a one-shot "One slope / Two different slopes" choice (`once` mode), with Check refused, visibly, until a choice is made. After Check: Point x, the full ladder, the stages Ruler, Tangent and Slope curve, the presets Hinge (ReLU at 0), V (|x| at 0) and Smooth (x² at 0), and on Hinge and V the "Lines under the corner" switch with a "Line slope s" slider, whose readout says whether s·x stays on or below the curve (exactly when the left slope ≤ s ≤ the right slope).
   - The point is ink, not ochre; the x slider and the presets aren't rendered before Check (no refusal sentence); `handleLabels` is omitted.
3. **Exact arithmetic.** Every curve is a piecewise polynomial with Fraction coefficients; the derivative comes from the coefficients and a corner is a boundary where two pieces' derivatives differ (`validateFunction` checks contiguity, continuity and the corners at import). x is a half-step index, h an index on a ladder that never holds 0, the guess a quarter-step index. Every number is printed in full with `boardText`'s `decimalText` at 9 places (the widest values are 3.381754501 and −0.006754501), which throws on a value that doesn't terminate; no board string uses "≈". `boardMath`'s LCM add, `subtractFractions` and `divideFractions` (a negative divisor's sign moves to the numerator) carry every left ruler. Floats arrive in week 3 with their own formatter.
4. **Square planes.** Every view is square in pixels (the plane is capped at 400 px, one column on every width, as on VectorBoard2D). x², ReLU and |x| use the equal-scale view [−2.5, 2.5] × [−0.5, 4.5]; x³ ([−2, 2] × [−4, 4]) and Loss ([−0.5, 6.5] × [−0.5, 10.5], axes named "w" and "loss") declare unequal scales, numbered every 1 across and every 2 up, and are drawn with `preserveAspectRatio={false}` so the edge test matches what is drawn. A drawn point exactly on the view's edge counts as on the board; a ruler end strictly outside gets the edge sentence ("The ruler's other end, (3, 9), is off the board.").
5. **Commit before reveal.** Before Check no string says "tangent", "derivative", "close in on", "Miss", "′" or "Slope curve", and none gives the slope 2; `check-boards` scans both halves of every spoken pair, every value text and every announcement over 1,224 covered states of `ruler-to-tangent` and 36 of `corner`, exempting only the guess slider's own value text and its commit. On `corner`, nothing says "no single slope", "No tangent", "however short" or "subgradient" before Check.
6. **Table view** ([0017](0017-table-view-fold.md)): one table, "h | Rise | Slope", or "h | Slope | Miss" once a tangent is drawn. The row header holds only h, with " (shown)" on the ruler the plane draws; line B names the ruler's other end. Caption "Rulers of length h from x = 1 on y = x²". To fit the tray's 255 px at 320 px, the row header wraps " (shown)" under h, the gaps are 0.75rem and the last column has none; measured, the widest rows (S-curve at x = −1.5, h = −0.001: "−0.006754501", "6.754501", and "0.004501 above") fit at 254 px.
7. **Color.** Ochre is one element at a time: the corner line while its switch is on, else the tangent when drawn, else the ruler. `planeMarks` returns every drawn mark with its color, line style and weight, and `check-boards` enumerates every reachable state of both setups and asserts exactly one ochre mark, ink labels, no tangent while the corner line is on (so the plane never holds two solid straight 3 px lines), and the line styles forced colors rely on: curve solid 2.5 px, ruler dashed 2.5 px, tangent and corner line solid 3 px, rise and run legs muted and dotted. Every segment is the shared `Segment` from `planeLines.tsx`. No pink or blue: these curves aren't statistics. On later pages the sigmoid is "sigmoid(x)", never σ.
8. **Slice mode is deferred to week 4, model only now.** `hill.ts` holds the hill 8 − x² − 2y², its east and north slices as ordinary curves, the partial slopes (−2x, −4y) and the contour ellipses x² + 2y² = 8 − c, all asserted. The slice board (map, linked slice plane, question and copy) is built the week before the partial derivatives page, from that page's needs; this supersedes the week-2 scope line "FunctionCurve with slice mode" for the board itself, following the model-now, board-later pattern used for NormBall and the regression board.

## Consequences
- Heights at 375 px with the table folded. The openings were measured on the lab page's server render (the plane at its 343 px fallback): 904 px for `ruler-to-tangent` and 920 px for `corner`; with the hydrated plane at 325 px that is about 886 and 902 px. After Check, estimated from the measured rows: about 1,020 px on Tangent (Bowl), about 1,105 px on Slope curve (the strip is a quarter of the plane, about 81 px), and about 1,130 px on `corner` with Lines under the corner on. All are over the 480 px target, as on VectorBoard2D: the square plane (340 px) and a tray that is the keyboard path take most of it. Levers not taken: a plane under square (the x² view needs five units of height at equal scales), folding the tray. Possible later trims: fewer presets, or the slope curve as a switch only on pages that teach it.
- The board's drawing (the Mafs plane and the strip, the label placement and the hydrated heights) hasn't been checked in a browser yet: the dev pane used for this build couldn't hydrate islands. The browser pass at 320 px, 375 px and desktop, in both themes, forced colors and print, is owed before the derivatives page ships.
- Page changes for `calculus/derivatives`, decided here under the board-wide rules:
  - P1: "The dot is where you sit; the dashed ochre line is the ruler." (the point is ink).
  - P2: no `handleLabels`; the Point slider is the keyboard path, and h is a slider over the ladder, not a radio group.
  - P3: no x refusal sentence; the x slider and the presets aren't rendered before Check.
  - P4: the switch is "Table view", and the table has no Run or Other end column (line B names the other end).
  - P5: Slide covers seats from x = 1 to x = 2, not 0 to 2.
  - P6: the everyday section must not state the answer before the board: all three mentions of 2 ("answers under 2", "land just under 2", "squeezed between the two: 2") go; Check gives 2.
  - P7: "Lines under the corner" is built, so the subgradients fold keeps its board text.
  - P8: the S-curve's x range is −1.5 to 1.5, not −2 to 2.
  - P9: Loss's direction sentence ("Slope −6: a step to the right lowers the loss.") appears only in the Tangent and Slope curve stages, where slope as a derivative is taught.
- Still owed: the NVDA/VoiceOver pass over the spoken forms ("y equals x squared", "minus") and the hidden handle, and Windows high contrast.

## Alternatives considered
- An HTML/SVG board: it would rebuild dragging, coordinates and curve sampling; a continuous plane is the Mafs side of [0011](0011-eventspace-html-board.md)'s split.
- A mode of NormalDistribution or DistributionBoard: their models are densities and weighted outcomes, and pink and blue would leak onto curves that aren't statistics.
- A tilt-the-ruler-until-it-touches world on (x − 4)²/4: the page's x² world, takeaways and code already use x².
- Floats everywhere: the ruler ladder is the lesson, and floats print rounding artifacts.
- A radio group for h: 12 options wrap to three or four rows at 375 px; a slider over the ladder index costs one row.
- A draggable ruler end: a pointer can't pick 0.001 apart from 0.01, and the slider is the complete path.
- Building the slice board and the later marks now: strings pinned before their pages exist get rewritten.
