# 0014: VectorBoard2D, a Mafs board for vectors

Status: accepted, 2026-11-24

## Context
The vectors and dot product pages need one board for arrows on a plane: two trips that add tip to tail, a vector and its multiples, a push and its part along a track, and the dot product and angle of two arrows. A vector lives on a continuous plane, so this is a Mafs board, the side of decision 0011's split that EventSpace and DistributionBoard don't cover. It is the first Mafs board with a goal and Check, the first with several vectors on one plane, and the first Mafs board built on the shared guided modules of decision 0013.

## Decision
- **One board, picked by a `setup` id**, as on the HTML boards: `trips-add` and `trips-scale` for the vectors page, `cart-push` and `align` for the dot product page. All math and copy live in pure modules (`vectorBoard/model.ts`, `setups.ts`, `state.ts`, `describe.ts`) checked by `npm run check:boards`.
- **Handles snap to whole numbers.** Every constraint rounds to the grid, then clamps each component to an interval. A constraint never leaves a hole in the grid (Mafs' keyboard loop would jump over it), so one arrow-key press always moves one unit. Sums, scalings and dot products are integers, and the exact angles (0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180°) come from integer tests on the dot and cross products. Only lengths and other angles use "≈": "√13 ≈ 3.61", "about 94.4°".
- **Everything stays on the guaranteed view, x and y in [−5, 5].** On `trips-add`, a moves only where a + b stays on the board, and b keeps its own entries within ±5 so the "b first" route fits too. On `trips-scale`, a's entries stay within ±2, so c a stays within ±4. On `align`, b's entries stay within ±4, which keeps b's shadow along any a within 4.83 of the center; `check:boards` checks every whole-number pair. On `cart-push`, the tilted-track preset can throw the push's shadow past the edge when the push is near (5, 5); the drawn shadow then stops at the edge, the line into the rails (past the edge too) and its label are left out, and the readout says "The shadow runs past the edge of the board." `validateSetup` refuses a setup whose openings or presets would leave the view, and runs at import, so a bad setup fails the build.
- **Two keyboard paths for every handle.** Each Mafs handle has a name from `handlesFor(state)` ("Tip of vector a. Its position is in the table below"), passed to `Board`'s `handleLabels`. The coordinate table under the plane is the second path: a native table whose movable rows have − / + buttons in normal tab order, grouped per cell ("Move a east or west") because a table row can't be a group. Screen readers in browse mode don't pass arrow keys to an SVG handle, so the table is the dependable path. A handle that reaches an edge says so when the interaction ends ("a is at the right edge of the board.").
- **Commit before reveal.** Every setup is a goal setup. `trips-add`, `trips-scale` and `cart-push` use Check with retries and "Show the answer" after two misses; `align` is a one-shot sign guess (Positive, Zero or Negative). Before Check, a and b are locked where they are the question (`trips-add`, `align`), with no Mafs handle at all and refused steppers that say why; the a + b row, the shadow, the angle, the presets and the qualitative readouts aren't rendered. `check:boards` scans every pre-Check sentence that doesn't echo the reader's own guess for the answer's words.
- **Color for several vectors on one plane.** Vectors are told apart by label and line style (a solid, b dashed, other routes muted dashed, component legs dotted), never by hue. Ochre marks exactly one element per state (`ochreElement`): the guess ring and then a + b, the scaled vector c a, or the shadow after Check. Pink and blue never appear: a vector is not μ or σ, and `check:boards` scans the board's files for the μ and σ colors. Handles are ink dots. A zero vector is a small ring labeled "zero vector", never an arrowhead (Mafs points a zero-length arrow's head east).
- **Notation in board strings** follows `src/data/notation.mjs` in plain Unicode: ‖a‖ for a vector's length, |c| kept for a number, "·" only for the dot product (a scalar multiple is written "c a" or "2 × a"), û for a unit track. `check:boards` fails on single bars around a vector or a "·" scalar multiple.
- **Stages on `trips-add`:** Trips (steps east and north, in words, with no numbers on the axes), Arrows (numbered axes and coordinates) and Lists (column vectors and sums component by component). Lists waits for Check. `trips-scale` offers Arrows and Lists; the dot product setups have no stages.
- **Column vectors are HTML**, two stacked numbers with bracket sides drawn in CSS, not KaTeX (decision 0007). The brackets are notation, not a decorative rule (decision 0006).
- **The plane is square** (`aspect-ratio: 1`, at most 440px), and Mafs keeps its default "contain" aspect ratio, so a right angle looks like one. A ResizeObserver gives Mafs the measured width as its height; the CSS reserves the square before hydration.
- **`Board` refreshes handle names when a change adds or removes a handle** (Check swaps the guess for two handles): the labels are stored in a layout effect, and the handles are described again whenever the labels change.

## Consequences
- Later vector pages (span, linear independence, orthogonality, embeddings) add setups to this board rather than new boards.
- **The board breaks the "at most about 480px tall" limit in [foundations.md](../design-system/foundations.md).** Measured on the lab page, `trips-add` is about 865px tall before Check and 1130px after it at a 1280px viewport, and about 890px and 1320px at 375px; the other setups run from about 740px to 970px. The square plane alone is up to 440px, and the coordinate table under it is the dependable keyboard path, so the two can't share 480px, and the content column is never wide enough to put the table beside the plane. Plane, readouts, table and tray therefore stack in one column. Whether to accept this, or to trade the table for a collapsible "Show as table" fold, is the owner's call.
- The "product" miss on `trips-add` (a guess of (3, 6)) can't be reached on the opening, because (3, 6) is off the board. Its sentence is kept and pinned in `check:boards` for a later setup that can reach it.
- The manual pass the definition of done asks for (NVDA and VoiceOver on handles and steppers, Windows high contrast) is still to do; forced colors map the board's ink, muted ink and ochre to system colors, because SVG strokes aren't forced. The other Mafs boards share that gap.

## Alternatives considered
- **A custom SVG or HTML plane without Mafs.** Full control of the aspect ratio, but it rebuilds drag, keyboard handling and coordinate transforms that Mafs provides, and it goes against decision 0011's split.
- **Four tray sliders (a x, a y, b x, b y) as the keyboard path.** They don't fit at 375px beside the scale slider and the buttons, and a slider splits one point into two unrelated controls. The table doubles as the "Show as table" view.
- **A separate similarity board for the dot product.** The dot product and similarity setups share one model, so they are setups of this board.
- **Clipping every shadow and saying so.** Simpler constraints, but the shadow is the taught element on `align`, so it stays fully on the board there. Only the tilted cart track, a preset that shows the length effect, uses the sentence.
- **A walking animation for the sum.** Left out: the reveal is instant, which also needs no reduced-motion case.
