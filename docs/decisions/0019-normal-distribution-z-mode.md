# 0019: NormalDistribution z mode

Status: accepted, 2026-11-25.

## Context
The z-scores worked example compares a 140 cm nine-year-old (class mean 134 cm, standard deviation 8 cm) with a 150 cm eleven-year-old (mean 144 cm, standard deviation 12 cm). Both stand 6 cm above their class's mean, so the question "who is taller for their age?" is answered by z = 6 / 8 = 0.75 against z = 6 / 12 = 0.5. The page's picture is a second ruler: standardizing relabels the ruler under a curve and keeps the area. The published `NormalDistribution` bell, on the home page and the Normal distribution page, drags μ and σ and moves the curve. It must render and behave exactly as before.

## Decision
- **`mode="z"` with a `setup`, on the existing board.** `NormalDistribution` takes `{ mode?: "bell" | "z"; setup?: string }`. Its default export is a dispatcher with no hooks: `resolveNormalProps` (in `normalDistribution/props.ts`) returns the bell for no props or `mode="bell"`, or the z mode with a known setup, and throws, naming the prop, for anything else (an unknown key such as `presets`, a bad mode, a setup without `mode="z"`, an unknown setup). Keys whose value is `undefined` and `children` are ignored, because Astro's client may pass `children` at hydration. The bell's body is now `BellBoard`, unchanged; its constants, `gaussian` and `describeState` moved to `normalDistribution/bell.ts` and are pinned in `check-boards`. `clamp`, `edgeSafeLabel`, `sigmaSide` and `sigmaFromHandleX` stay beside `BellBoard`.
- **The bell's server render is byte-identical.** Built with `npx astro build --outDir node_modules/.zmode/before` before any change and again after, the `<astro-island>` for `NormalDistribution` in `index.html` and `statistics/normal-distribution/index.html` is equal once `component-url`, `renderer-url`, `before-hydration-url` and `uid` are removed. The bell hydrates with no console errors, its handles drag and its sliders move it.
- **The z board is a lazy chunk.** The bell sits outside any Suspense boundary; `ZBoard` loads with `React.lazy` inside one, as DistributionBoard's family and density boards do (0018), and renders in full on the server. Measured on the production build (gzip level 9 of the island's static import graph): the bell pages go from 36.7 KB to 38.4 KB, the dispatcher, the prop guard and Vite's preload helper; a z page adds 10.3 KB, of which `ZBoard` itself is 6.4 KB and the rest shared board modules. Loaded statically, the bell pages would have grown by 9.4 KB.
- **One fixed curve, two rulers.** The view is in z units (x from −4 to 4, y from −0.125 to 0.47, 240 px tall, about 403 px per unit), so presets relabel the rulers and never change the curve's shape. The ruler in the group's units sits nearest the axis (about 18 px under it) and the z ruler under it (about 38 px), as on the page's picture. The standard preset has no unit row, so pressing it reads as "the cm ruler goes, the z ruler stays". Ruler labels are 14 px; their captions ("cm", "z") sit at x = −3.75, which keeps "108" clear of "cm" at 320 px.
- **One setup, `taller-for-age`**, with three presets: `nine-140` (mean 134, sd 8, step 1 cm, opening 140 cm), `eleven-150` (144, 12, 1 cm, 150 cm) and `standard-075` (0, 1, step 1/20, opening 0.75). `validateSetup` runs at import and throws on a non-whole or non-positive sd, a non-whole mean, a step that doesn't divide sd or isn't whole on a measured preset, an opening off the grid or beyond 3σ, fewer than 2 or more than 4 presets, a duplicate or unknown id, an opening preset outside the list, waiting for Check or not one of the two children, a child that isn't a measured preset, children at different distances or with equal z, and children that don't match what the verdicts say (the second taller in centimeters, the first taller for their age). The verdict copy is this setup's; the validation checks its two facts rather than claiming generality.
- **Commit before reveal.** On a fixed z view the marker's position is z, so the drawing would answer the question. The board opens on the nine-year-olds alone with the cm ruler, the curve, the μ guide, the σ span, the ochre marker, the slider and the readouts "Nine-year-olds: mean μ = 134 cm, standard deviation σ = 8 cm." and "140 cm is 6 cm above the mean.". The reader answers "Who is taller for their age: the 140 cm nine-year-old or the 150 cm eleven-year-old?" with a native radio group and a one-shot Check (`recordCheck` in `once` mode); Check is refused with "Pick who is taller for their age first." until a radio is picked. Until Check there is no z row, no z text, no Shade switch, no z or share table row and no preset group (only one preset is allowed, and one button is no choice). One pure predicate, `revealed(state)`, is the gate every component and describe.ts reads. `check-boards` sweeps all 49 pre-Check values × 4 guesses × the table open or not, scanning the readouts, every announcement those states can produce, the refusal, the fold body, every table cell and the slider's text for "z =", "z is", "Φ", "Phi", "%", "0.75", "0.5", "Eleven-year-olds" and "standard normal". The fold is not the gate (0017).
- **Exact numbers.** A value is an integer step index; its distance and z are `boardMath` fractions, and every exact decimal goes through `boardText`'s `decimalText` (the value lines, the verdicts, the table, the slider's text on the standard preset). z reads "= 0.875" when it terminates within 3 places and "≈ 0.58" otherwise, with Φ's argument as "7/12". Φ comes from an all-positive erf series (at most 33 terms at |z| ≤ 3) and is within 4e−16 of the `math.erf` form; the tests match Python within 1e−12. All 243 reachable states (49 + 73 + 121) are at least 1e−7 from a 3- or 4-decimal rounding boundary (the tightest, z = ±3, is 1.02e−7), and no share prints as 0% or 100% (Φ(−3) is "0.0013", about 0.1%). "Exactly 50%" and "Φ(0) = 0.5" are chosen when the fraction z is 0, never from the float.
- **The ChalkNote** "same shape, new ruler" shows on the standard preset only, above the plot in normal flow (`.ms-normal-z .ms-chalk-note`, as EventSpace does): over the plot it covered the μ label and the peak at 320, 375 and 1280 px. It adds about 32 px there.
- **Color.** Pink: the dashed μ guide and its "μ" label above the peak. Blue: the solid σ span at the inflection height and its "σ" label beside the span's right end. Ochre, one element: the value marker and its handle. Ink: the curve, the shading (fill 0.2) and the cm row; muted ink: the z row. The label positions are swept over all 243 states so no marker runs through either label. In forced colors `.ms-normal-z` maps the ink, muted ink, ochre, board, grid and axis tokens as `.ms-vec` does, plus μ and σ to CanvasText; they stay apart by dashed against solid and by their labels.
- **Accessibility.** The chart is `aria-hidden`; the readouts, announcements and the table behind Table view are its equivalents. The tray slider is the handle's keyboard path ("Height", 1 cm per arrow, 5 cm per Page Up or Down; "Value z", 0.05 and 0.25), so the handle is out of the tab order. Lines whose spoken form differs ("z is 6 divided by 8, which is 0.75", "Phi of minus 7 over 12 is about 0.2798") render as a visible span and a screen-reader span (`SpokenText`), the focused verdict included. A passed Check announces nothing, because focus moves to the verdict and it is read there; a guess announces nothing beyond the native radio. Buttons and switches announce at once; a slider commit or a drag end announces the value line (and the share line while Shade is on). A late slider commit goes through the shared slider guard (0018), so a preset or Reset pressed since keeps it silent and a fresh key press speaks again; `check-boards` asserts the three cases.

## Consequences
- Heights at 375 px with the table folded, measured on the lab page on 2026-11-25: 737 px at the opening (646 px once a radio is picked and the refusal note goes), 917 px after Check, 994 px with Shade on, 1,018 px on `eleven-150` with Shade on, 1,001 px on `standard-075` with Shade on (its ChalkNote included); about 1,340 px with the table open there. At 1280 px: 639 px at the opening, 701 px after Check with Shade on. The canvas is 240 px (the bell's is 360 px). The folded table fits the tray at 320 px (255 px for "≈ 0.7202, about 72.0%").
- Every state is over the 480 px target. The tray takes the room: the question and Check before, the three preset buttons on three rows and the verdict after. Levers, cheapest for the learner first, none taken: shorter preset labels ("Age 9, 140 cm"), about −48 px after Check, which would also change the "Already showing …" sentences and the page; a shorter verdict (the value line already gives the nine-year-old's z); a 200 px canvas, −40 px with cramped ruler rows; dropping the slider and handle, −69 px, which loses the keyboard path the page describes.
- Page-spec corrections for the z-scores page: import `<NormalDistribution client:visible mode="z" setup="taller-for-age" />`; the board opens without any z; the standard group line reads "Standard normal: mean μ = 0, standard deviation σ = 1." (spoken without "μ =" and "σ ="), like the other two group lines; the standard value line is "On the standard normal the value is its own z-score: z = 0.75."; the switch is "Table view"; the ChalkNote "same shape, new ruler" shows on the standard preset only.
- Checked on the lab page on 2026-11-25 (dev server, Chrome, light and dark themes), before Check and after Check with Shade on:
  - **Forced colors** (emulated): the curve, the μ guide, the σ span and both rulers draw in system colors, and the shading stays visible without an override. CanvasText at fill 0.2 against Canvas is 1.61:1 in light and 1.66:1 in dark; the same shading in normal colors is 1.44:1 in light and 1.77:1 in dark. The share line gives the value either way.
  - **Print preview**: before Check the board prints the cm ruler, the two group lines, the height and the folded table's four rows, with no z anywhere; after Check it adds the z ruler, the shading, the verdict and the z and share rows. Both themes print in dark ink on white. The pass found that the question, the presets and EventSpace's cup buttons still printed on every board, because their own `display` rules come later in `components.css` than the print block; the print block now scopes them under `.ms-board`.
  - **axe** (axe-core 4.13; WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA, plus best practices), before Check and after Check with Shade on and the table open: no violation in the board. The only one on the page is `image-redundant-alt` on the site logo in the header. The `aria-hidden` wrapper has no focusable descendant in either state, and `aria-hidden-focus` doesn't fire.
- Not yet checked: the NVDA/VoiceOver pass (whether the Mafs text inside the `aria-hidden` wrapper is ever read twice), and the z board's production server render inside Suspense, since its only instance so far is a draft page that production builds leave out.

## Alternatives considered
- **The page spec's `presets` / `initialPreset` props.** A question and its gate must travel with the presets; a `setup` id keeps them together.
- **Opening on the nine-year-old with z showing.** Pressing the eleven-year-old would answer the prediction before the reader commits.
- **A typed z guess.** Computing z is the page's worked examples; the board's prediction is the comparison.
- **A moving bell in the group's units.** The shape would change between presets and hide that only the labels change.
- **Ochre shading.** One ochre element per board; the shading is ink.
- **A separate `NormalZ.tsx` island.** It would duplicate `gaussian` and the drawing constants.
- **A generation counter in the z reducer for late slider commits.** The shared `sliderGuard` (0018) is the same rule, already tested; the z board calls `interrupt` on every non-slider action.
- **Loading the z board statically.** 9.4 KB gzip more on two published pages that never use it.
