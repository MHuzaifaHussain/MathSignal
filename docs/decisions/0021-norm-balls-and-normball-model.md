# 0021: Norm balls on VectorBoard2D, and the NormBall model

Status: accepted, 2026-11-26. Amends [0014](0014-vector-board.md). The `city-walk` copy and every NormBall number are provisional: they are re-pinned when the vector norms page spec (week 3) and the ridge and lasso, Lagrange multiplier and weight decay page specs (week 9) are written. NormBall's UI (setups, reducer, copy, plane, table) follows once those week-8 and week-9 page specs exist.

## Context
The vector norms page measures one vector three ways (L1, L2, L∞) and shows the shape of every point one length from the origin. Three later pages (ridge and lasso, Lagrange multipliers, weight decay as a Gaussian prior) need constraint geometry: loss contours around a best fit, a penalty that pulls the weights in, a fence they must stay inside, and a path a point slides along. On the norms page the reader moves an arrow tip, as on every vector page. On the constraint pages the reader moves a penalty, or a point that may only slide along a fence; the weights are computed, never dragged. Building the constraint board's UI now would mean writing its copy six weeks before its pages exist, which is how week 1's boards had to be reworked when their page specs came later.

## Decision
1. **Split by what the reader moves.** Norm balls are a VectorBoard2D setup, `city-walk` (kind `norms`), because the reader moves an arrow tip. Constraint geometry is a separate board, NormBall, because the reader moves a penalty or slides along a fence and the board computes the weights. This amends 0014's "later vector pages add setups to this board" to "later vector pages whose reader moves arrow tips".
2. **One shared geometry module, `vectorBoard/norms.ts`**, used by both: `normParts` (always exact Fractions: ‖v‖₁, ‖v‖₂ kept squared, ‖v‖∞), `ballSide` (inside, on or outside, by exact comparison, ‖v‖₂ against r²), `ballOutline` (diamond tips on the axes, square corners at (±r, ±r), or a circle) and `latticeOnBall` (16 whole-number corners for L1 at r = 4, 12 for L2 at r = 5, 32 for L∞ at r = 4). `boardMath` gains `absoluteFraction`, `fractionSign` and `maxFraction`, each asserted, and stays import-free.
3. **`city-walk`** opens with the pin at (3, 4), home at the origin, v as an ink arrow and the walking route as an ochre dotted staircase (east leg, then north leg). The reader may move the pin anywhere in ±5 (handle and the "Your pin" row of the step strip).
   - Before Check the board says only the walk ("Walking the streets: 3 + 4 = 7 blocks."). A one-shot guess (`once`), "Corners 4 blocks' walk from home make a", offers "Circle", "Diamond: tips on the streets" and "Square: sides along the streets". The options are named by orientation, because a diamond is a square turned 45°, and a reader who picks Square meaning the tilted one must not be marked wrong. The Square verdict says so: "A diamond is a square turned on its corner. The walking corners make that one, with its tips on the streets, not the one with its sides along them." Check is refused, with a visible reason, until a shape is picked.
   - After Check: the ochre ball at length 4 with its whole-number corners as muted dots; the stages Blocks and Numbers (the ids `trips` and `arrows`, relabeled per setup by `stageLabel`; Numbers waits for Check); "Shape for" (Walk, Bird, King, All three; L1, L2, L∞, All three in Numbers); a "Length r" slider from 1 to 5; and the presets Friend's house, Straight street, Diagonal and Nested shapes. "All three" draws the three outlines in ink, diamond solid, circle dashed, square dotted, each labeled, with no ochre element. The square at r = 5 sits on the view's edge and is drawn (the 0.5 padding holds it), so the slider runs to 5 and no preset rule forbids it.
   - "Walk" always means the shortest walk and "king" the fewest king moves; the king's shape line says "every corner 4 king moves from home at the fewest", never "every square", which would mix the chess square with the shape.
   - A preset's lesson ("On the diagonal the three measures differ most.") shows under the shape line while the preset's values are untouched; a press announces the preset's whole sentence, numbers and the pin's side included. The readouts keep the numbers and the side, so the visible lesson leaves them out.
   - The live region says ‖v‖₁, ‖v‖₂ and ‖v‖∞ as "the L1 norm of v", "the L2 norm of v" and "the L-infinity norm of v" before the generic "the length of" rule.
4. **Commit before reveal.** Before Check no string outside the three option labels and the reader's own guess echo says "diamond", "circle", "square", "bird", "flying", "king", "√" or "‖". `check:boards` scans every board string, table cell, announcement and refusal at all 121 pins, with each guess picked; words are matched whole and in any case ("walking" holds "king"). The coordinate row behind the strip leaves the pin's straight length empty, since it is the answer.
5. **The folded table is its own component, `NormTable`** (Measure, How, Length: Walk (L1) |3| + |4| 7; Bird (L2) √(3² + 4²) 5; King (L∞) max(|3|, |4|) 4), not extra rows under `CoordinateTable`'s columns. Before Check it has only the walk row. It reuses `.ms-vec-table` styles, so print and forced colors treat it like the coordinate table, and it joins the Table view no-controls scan. Its opening sentence is the shared prefix plus a body ("It has one row, the walk, with how it is counted and its length.").
6. **NormBall's model now, exact** (`normBall/model.ts`). The loss is ½ Σ hᵢ (wᵢ − cᵢ)² with a diagonal curvature, the decorrelated-features case of Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville (2016, §7.1), so ridge is hᵢ cᵢ ÷ (hᵢ + α) and lasso soft-thresholds each weight by α ÷ hᵢ, and every weight on the α grid (0 to 6 in steps of 0.5) is a fraction. The forms are pinned in the module: ridge and weight decay minimize L + (α ÷ 2)‖w‖₂², lasso L + α‖w‖₁. The `touch` path is w₁ + w₂ = 3 from (0, 3) to (3, 0), one side of the diamond only (the full diamond has a second tangency at (−2, −1)); the point is a step index 0 to 12, so no float reaches a Fraction. λ uses the additive Lagrangian L + λ g with g = w₁ + w₂ − 3, so λ = 2 at (2, 1). `validateModel` runs at import and refuses a non-positive curvature, a center or any grid α's weights off the board, a `touch` ring that crosses the path twice or touches it (every level from the path's lowest loss, 3, up to the lower corner loss, 4.5; so the rings are 1, 2 and 8, never 4.5), a path without exactly one parallel point, and an arrow tip off the board. `check:boards` pins every preset's weights, losses and fence sizes (5.5, 243/50, 0.75, 19/9, 9, 1252/225; √29 ÷ 5 and 13/15), every grid α against the closed forms, the 13 path losses, the slide hints, the single parallel point and the KKT check at the lasso corner.
7. **Color.** Ochre is one element (`ochreElement`): the route before Check, then the chosen ball, none for "All three". Ink arrows and outlines, muted corner dots. No pink or blue: a norm is not μ or σ, and the board's files are scanned for both.

## Consequences
- **Height (estimated at 375 px, table folded; to be measured in the browser pass):** `city-walk` about 800 px before Check and about 1,120 px after it, so about 320 px and 640 px over the 480 px goal. The square plane (up to 343 px at 375 px) is the floor; the rest is the strip, the guess and, after Check, the stage choice, shape choice, slider and presets, which are the keyboard path and stay visible. NormBall's estimates, for its UI later: `fence` about 700 and 870 px, `decay` about 760 px, `touch` about 900 px.
- **NormBall's UI is not built.** Still to do with its page specs: `normBall/setups.ts` (with the "a guess's correct answer is unique" rule), `state.ts`, `describe.ts` with its forbidden-string scans, `NormPlane.tsx` (contours as Mafs `Ellipse`, the fence, the `touch` path drawn as one labeled segment, the path steppers reusing `VectorSteppers`' button), the α slider left out before Check rather than refused, the folded tables (values only in cells, so they fit the tray's 256 px at 320 px), the lab page and the docs.
- **Registries owed:** `notation.mjs` entries for ‖v‖₁, ‖v‖∞, the penalty α (noting it is not the smoothing count or a significance level) and λ; w₁ and w₂ go in the existing `weight-vector` note; `references.mjs` gains the regularization chapter. The pages add them; the board strings already use the registry's spoken forms.
- **Screen readers:** the choice labels "L1", "L2" and "L∞" have no sr-only spoken names yet (TrayChoice takes plain labels); confirm in the NVDA and VoiceOver pass that "L∞" is read as "L infinity".
- The manual pass (NVDA, VoiceOver, Windows high contrast, 320, 375 and 1280 px with the table open) is owed with the other boards.

## Alternatives considered
- **One NormBall board for all four pages.** A second vector board with its own handle and stepper code for the norms page, against 0013 and 0014, for no reader gain.
- **Everything as VectorBoard2D setups.** Its state would gain a penalty, a fence and contour levels that its other setups never use.
- **Round loss contours.** Exact too, but the lasso corner then looks like plain "nearest point", and the elongated ring the ridge and lasso page needs disappears.
- **Tilted contours with "≈" readouts.** They break the exact-number rule, and the L2 solution needs a numeric root finder in the island.
- **Building NormBall's UI in week 2.** Its copy would be written before its page specs exist; the owner ruled model now, UI later.
- **A "Show the answer" retry on the shape guess.** Any shape reveals the answer once picked, so the guess is one-shot, like `align` and `span-shape`.
